LEGAL / PRIVACY

Your privacy
matters to us.

This policy explains how eSenceDesk handles information shared through our website, enquiry forms and business communications.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. Information we collect

When you contact eSenceDesk, we may collect your name, email address, company or website, phone number, project details, language requirements and any information you choose to include in your message.

2. How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, prepare quotations, plan translation, localization, subtitling, content, publishing, software, SEO and data services, manage projects, communicate with clients and improve our website and services.

3. Project and service data

Documents and content you provide may contain business, linguistic or technical information. We use that material only as reasonably necessary to scope, perform, review and deliver the requested service and to communicate with you about the project.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted team members, contractors or technology providers only when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, operate our website, process communications or meet legal obligations.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. We retain information only for as long as it is needed for the relevant business, contractual, accounting, legal or security purpose.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and preference storage to keep the site functional and remember your language choice. We do not use third-party machine-translation widgets for the language selector.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or object to certain processing. To request information or raise a privacy question, contact us using the details below.
