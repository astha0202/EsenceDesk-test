Translation & Localization

Translation is the communication from one language to another language. Translation refers to written information, whereas interpretation refers to spoken information. The purpose of translation is to convey the original tone and intent of a message, taking into account cultural and regional differences between source and target languages.

Translation has been used by humans for centuries, beginning after the appearance of written literature. Modern-day translators use sophisticated tools and technologies to accomplish their work, and rely heavily on software applications to simplify and streamline their tasks.

Organizations around the world, encompassing a multitude of sectors, missions, and mandates, rely on translation for content as diverse as product labels, technical documentation, user reviews, promotional materials, annual reports, and much, much more.

Typical content
  • Product labels
  • Technical documentation
  • User reviews
  • Promotional materials
  • Annual reports
  • Multilingual content

Subtitling & Media

Subtitling is a type of audiovisual translation that has its own specifications, rules and criteria. This type of translation belongs to “subordinate translation”.

It is a translation that has restrictions of time and space which directly affect the final result. The translation depends on these parameters and does not only consist of translating the textual context, but also supporting the image and audio, with determined time and space.

The space available is limited to 1–2 lines of subtitles which are usually placed and generally centred at the bottom of the screen. Each line cannot contain more than 35 characters.

Focus
  • Time & space restrictions
  • Textual context
  • Image & audio support
  • 1–2 subtitle lines
  • Maximum 35 characters per line

eBook Conversion

An eBook is an electronic version of a traditional print book that can be read by using a personal computer or by using an eBook reader. An eBook reader can be a software application for use on a computer or a book-sized computer used solely as a reading device.

Users can purchase an eBook on diskette or CD, but the most popular method is to purchase a downloadable file from a Website to be read from a computer or reading device. eBooks are popular because they enable options similar to paper books, including bookmarks, notes, highlights, selected text, built-in dictionaries and alterable font sizes and styles.

Formats
  • ePub
  • HTML
  • PDF
  • Mobi
  • Digital reading workflows
